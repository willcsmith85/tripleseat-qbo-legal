# PRIVACY POLICY
## Tripleseat to QuickBooks Integration Application

**Last Updated:** January 31, 2026

Urban Canvas LLC d/b/a Culinary Canvas ("Company," "We," "Us," or "Our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our Tripleseat to QuickBooks Integration application ("Application") handles your information.

---

## 1. INFORMATION WE ACCESS

### Data We Access (But Do Not Store)

The Application requires read access to:

**From Tripleseat:**
- Event information (names, dates, locations, statuses)
- Customer/account information
- Contact information
- Financial data (invoice amounts, line items, payments)
- Category and billing totals

**From QuickBooks Online:**
- Customer records
- Invoice records
- Item/service records
- Tax code information

**Important:** This data flows directly between your Tripleseat and QuickBooks accounts. We do not store, collect, or retain this data on our servers.

### Data We Store Locally

The Application stores minimal metadata on your local system:
- Tripleseat Event IDs (to prevent duplicate syncs)
- Sync timestamps
- Success/failure status of sync operations
- Configuration settings

This data is stored in a local database file (`sync_log.db`) on your computer and is NOT transmitted to us or any third party.

---

## 2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

The Application uses the accessed information solely to:

a) **Synchronize Data:** Transfer event and invoice data from Tripleseat to QuickBooks Online

b) **Prevent Duplicates:** Track which events have been synced to avoid creating duplicate invoices

c) **Error Reporting:** Identify and report synchronization errors via email notifications

d) **Validate Data:** Ensure data accuracy (tax calculations, category mappings)

---

## 3. INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

### We Do NOT Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your data with third parties, except as described below:

**Third-Party Services:**
- **Intuit (QuickBooks):** The Application transmits invoice data directly to your QuickBooks account via Intuit's API
- **Tripleseat:** The Application retrieves event data directly from your Tripleseat account via their API

**Legal Requirements:**
We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

### Data Transmission

All data transmission between Tripleseat and QuickBooks occurs directly through their respective APIs using:
- Secure HTTPS connections
- OAuth 2.0 authentication (QuickBooks)
- OAuth 1.0 authentication (Tripleseat)
- Industry-standard encryption

---

## 4. DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your information:

a) **Encryption:** All API communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption

b) **Authentication:** OAuth tokens are stored securely on your local system

c) **Access Control:** Only authorized users with valid Tripleseat and QuickBooks credentials can use the Application

d) **No Cloud Storage:** We do not store your financial data on any cloud servers

**Your Responsibility:**
- Keep your Tripleseat and QuickBooks credentials secure
- Protect the computer where the Application is installed
- Regularly update the Application when new versions are released

---

## 5. DATA RETENTION

**Local Sync Log:**
- Sync metadata is retained locally until you delete the Application
- You may delete the sync log database at any time
- No data is retained by us after Application removal

**Tripleseat & QuickBooks:**
- Data remains in your Tripleseat and QuickBooks accounts per their retention policies
- We do not control data retention in these third-party services

---

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

You have the right to:

a) **Access:** Review the local sync log database at any time

b) **Delete:** Remove the Application and all local data at any time

c) **Revoke Access:** Disconnect the Application from QuickBooks or Tripleseat through their respective platforms

d) **Modify:** Change configuration settings including what data is synchronized
